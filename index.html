Where the data comes from

    Reading the specs

    • Processor shows the exact chip (e.g. Core Ultra 9 275HX) when the store gave it, otherwise the family from the product title (e.g. Core i7).
    • Graphics marked “integrated” means the laptop or PC has no separate graphics card. Gaming, 3D and video editing work best with a dedicated card such as an RTX or Radeon RX.
    • Graphics power sorting uses a rough ranking from RTX 5080 at the top down to integrated graphics. It is a guide, not a benchmark.
    • Barebone mini PCs come without RAM or storage; budget for those separately.
